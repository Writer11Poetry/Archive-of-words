The Ruins · 07

Home

16 March 2026

I only craved a home
A place I didn’t feel like a stranger
A place where mirrors didn’t seem dangerous
A place where my heart could bleed
A place where my eyes could water
But I guess I wasn’t worthy enough for that

So I built quiet rooms in my head
Rooms that felt familiar
A place where silence held me gently
Because humans never could
They never will

I searched the rooms for warmth
But there was nothing but coldness
The echoes never answered
Even when I screamed
Maybe home was never meant for me…

So I stayed in the silent rooms
I learned the language of loneliness
Memorizing the cracks in the walls
Like they were the cracks in my mask

I searched for doors who weren’t there
I searched for me when there was just nothing
The white sterile walls look down at me
The only comfort I’ll ever feel

When will I be home?
Or was home never meant for me?

The feeling of emptiness and numbness became my home
Please don’t take that from me
Don’t take that from me
It’s the only thing
I’ve got left to lose

Let me keep it
Let me keep
The home
That was never one
But I pretended it was
