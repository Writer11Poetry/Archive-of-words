Burning candles

19 March 2026

I’m the burning candle on your cake
Even though I’m fake
I’m the burning candle that gives you warmth
But I sacrifice myself for your love

You searched for warmth in my arms
Just to forget your pain
But my body and soul are covered in scars
And my flames have long gone out

You search for a home
One that I cannot give
I’ve burned myself
Just to give you light

And with every flame that I give
Every flame that I ever gave
I slowly fade away
Until I’m nothing but wax

You watched me burn for you
Never saying “Thank you”
You watched me
Like my burn marks were something beautiful
But you never noticed
I was slowly disappearing for you

For your love
For your approval

I am afraid to be the only candle left
Standing on this cake

I melted into your hands
I shaped myself the way you wanted me too
But I knew
I knew, I’d never be whole again

But who cares about that?
So I chose you instead of me
I chose to keep burning
Until I disappeared

I knew that candles were never meant to burn forever
So I poured wax into myself, slowly poisoning myself
Made me look so perfect
So you’d stay even when there were no flames left

I’m still burning for you
But one day
Even my light will end

And when the darkness finally hits
Will you still see me?
Or was I never meant to be seen?
Was I just meant to give light to others?
To spend all my warmth until I turned cold?

And I know even with my flames I’m not enough
But what without them?
I’m just a worthless pile of wax

So I kept burning
Because losing myself
Was easier than losing you

I chose to dissolve
And the walls kept stretching, closing in around me

When my light finally goes out
You’ll finally see
I’m gone
There is nothing left to safe

Will you remember me?
Or just the warmth that I gave?

And I hope I haunt your dreams
Because you were the villain all along
But you painted the story like I was the villain

Maybe I am
But for now
I’m dissolving into smoke
