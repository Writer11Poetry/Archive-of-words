Defences

20 March 2026

I’ve tried to speak with you
But I just broke like glass
Are you even trying to understand
The silence I carry?

I’m fighting my thoughts again
With no way to escape
I’m tangled in my thoughts
I'm spinning right out of place

While others are waiting
For me to finally shut down

I'm tangled in my own reflection
Turning into nothing

I’ve tried to explain myself a thousand times
But my words shatter each fucking time

You look at me
Like you see the pieces I cannot show
The ones I tried to hide

You're looking at me
Like you're reading between the lines and words
I'm too scared to mean

I'm trying so hard
But I’m falling apart again

I'm losing my defences
The ones I’ve built to the sky

I'm losing the only thing
That’s left in my name
