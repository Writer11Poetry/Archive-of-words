The Ruins · 03

Promises and lies

14 March 2026

You promised me the world
But you just let me fall
You promised to help me up again
But you let me rot on the ground
You promised that I was never temporary
Now, I’m just a memory

When can you understand?
That my trust is already shattered
Something I’m slowly trying to repair
But my hands are already bleeding

Not only from the shards
I made them that way
A secret promise to myself
An escape
That one day I’ll get better
Until then
I’ll keep lying to myself
And let them bleed

“I’m okay” and “I’m fine”
Those are my favourite lies
“I love you” and “I hear you”
Those were yours
The lies I believed
Those that broke me
The ones that still make my hands shiver

I know you used me like a toy
You used me just for fun
Until I got boring
So you left me

I was alone
Alone with my thoughts
The ones that scare me
In a place I'm not in control
A place I’ll never find comfort in

Help
Help me
Even when I smile
Help me
Even though I’m just a kid
Help me
Even when I say I’m okay
Help me
Help
Help me please
Don’t lie to me
