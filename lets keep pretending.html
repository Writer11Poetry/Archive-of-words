Let's keep pretending

15 March 2026

Let’s just keep pretending that my story never ended
Let’s just keep pretending that I never gave up
Please, let’s keep it that way

I’m begging you to let go
To finally let me shatter
That’s all I ever wanted
To be so broken that people finally see
That the cracks were so big that everyone could see

Please break me once more
Please
Even though it hurts for a second
I’ll turn numb
Please hurt me more
So I have more reasons to cry about

And you know I hit rock bottom
When I don’t try to everyone anymore
I promise I won’t put them back together

I, myself was never meant to be whole
So let’s destroy the shards that aren’t completely broken

Maybe my silence will finally speak for me
Maybe then you’ll notice the sound of glass
Maybe breaking was the only honest thing about me

My story ended long ago
Maybe the story should have never started
Maybe
Maybe
Maybe

Until the stars fall down
I will remain broken
My cracks were the only truth I had
The stars keep shining
But I never will

Let’s keep pretending
Let’s keep it this way
Just one more starry night
Just one more night…
