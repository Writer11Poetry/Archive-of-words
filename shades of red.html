Static Heart · 01

Shades of red

?? March 2026

As I’m jumping from world to world
From planet to planet
I’m trying to forget and escape the worlds I’ve influenced
I’m trying to get others to forget me

But no matter how hard I try
I’m like the moon orbiting around them
They pull me in close

I try to get my distance
But I can’t leave this orbit
So we just keep it this way

We’re painting the stars in the sky
Even the bloody moon
Though we’re painting on different canvas
Your art shining on paper
And mine imprinted on my skin
With two different shades of red

You think that I’m just stupid
Painting my hands with the same red as you are
But you don’t see where it comes from

And my canvas slowly starts bleeding
Or was it blood all along
Until you realize
I chose this path

The path with the red
And you stare me in the eyes
But I look away
Just like I always do
You’re trying to figure me out
But my windows are closed

Every time I felt blue
I drowned it in red

And I know I’ve stayed on this path for way too long
With marks that I cannot wash away
I’ve tried washing away the red
But I was never quite successful

Maybe I wasn’t trying to escape
I was just trying to turn red

I’m still orbiting around you
But with the red dripping from my hands
So you pull me in even tighter

I'm bound to the worlds I'm orbiting
There is no escape
So let me sink in the canvas

Red
That’s what I’ve become
Even if you don’t see it

Now everything I touch
Turns the same shade
There is no other colour left
Just variations of what I am
