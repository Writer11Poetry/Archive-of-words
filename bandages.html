The Ruins · 06

Bandages

15 March 2026

I miss who I was
Before I learned how easily people leave

I tried to put on plasters
On my damaged skin
But I needed bandages

I tried to keep it together
But no matter how hard I tried
The strings
The ones you took
They were the thing that kept me together
Now I’m just a pile

Waiting to be stitched up
Or plastered together
Give me my bandages
They are like the air
The one I need to breathe

Fix me
Please
