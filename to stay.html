The Ruins · 01

To Stay

14 March 2026

I lost my self
When the silhouettes stopped the fight
And the ashes I’m made of
They are grey and dull
Maybe I’m the problem
The problem…

I’ve begged you to see me
Not just the smile
Not just what seemed real
Not just what you believed
I begged you to see me
With all my scars
With all my flaws
For once not be shamed for them
For once to be heard
For once to be seen

But you were never meant
To take off your blindfold
You were never ready to see me
So I just fold myself even smaller
I break all my bones
Just for you
For you
For you

Still you decide to look away
Like I am just smoke
Like I just drift through your hands
You’ve offered me lectures
When I desperately needed a hug

But in the end
I remember the fire
How it burnt me
How it turned me in to who I am today
…
Into a hollow shell
With a pretty face

Somewhere in those ruins
There is still a part of me that can’t leave
And that’s the hope I hold onto
The hope was my light
Not the one that burned
The one that stayed
That one thing I needed you to do
To stay
