The Ruins · 04

Why?

14 March 2026

Why is this world so cruel?
Why did I have to break?
Why was it me?
And I know this world isn’t fair
I know I’m in denial

The time moves on without me
I’m stuck with no hope at all
You let me burn in the ruins
Now, every day feels the same

The weeks pass
But I can’t even remember
It all feels the same
The same
The same
The same

When I’m with friends
I force myself to be happy
But deep down
There is this heavy sadness
Deep down I just want to weep out my eyes

All the emotion you’ll ever see
Will never be the real one
You’ll never see me like I will
I feel so empty
Like every part has been scooped out of me

I just want to be heard
Is it so hard?
Is it?

You tell me all your problems and secrets
But when is it my turn?
When can I be vulnerable?
I’ve long lost my pride
Standing in the shadows
Slowly blending in

Please
Just hear me
Please
And I’m begging you to stay
Just for once
Please
Stay just this one time
Please
I don’t want to be alone anymore
Please
Please
Please.
