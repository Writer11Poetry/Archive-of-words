Villain of the story

17 March 2026

I’m the villain of my story
Because who else am I to blame?
And I hate myself for all the things
I did to myself

I disgust myself so much
And I don’t even remember
How it feels to be happy

All I ever wanted was to be loved
But you were a traitor
And I like how you keep using me
You gave me a purpose
Because I was never able to give myself one

I loved your lies
The lies I believed
Though every lie was like a knife
Stabbing me in the back

I'm the villain of my story
Because I let you
Not because I wanted
But because I had too
