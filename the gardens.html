The Ruins · 09

The gardens

15 March 2026

I may genuinely don’t know when I was happy
Even when I smile
That’s why I try so hard to make others happy
I don’t want them to turn numb like me
Maybe happiness was just temporary

I watered the gardens of others
But let my own flowers die
They died from the shadows
They weren’t able to survive the dark
But I’m still here
So will I be able to?

Will the darkness consume me?
Or will I let it go?

My own garden has died
And I’m waiting for a day
Someone will see
That my flowers need water too
That they need light
Something I could never give
There was too much darkness inside of me

I was called selfish when I tried
So I let them die
Even the spiky thorns

Maybe one day
Someone will be brave enough
To walk into my ruins and water my plants
Someone will notice the cracks in the soil
A reflection of my own face

But what if my garden
Was never able to grow flowers?
And every drop of water
Makes me more dependent
It makes me manipulatable
And you knew how to use that

You cut off the roots of the plans

Maybe one day
I can learn how to water my own flowers
And my tears will come back as rain
My begging will be heard
And will return as light
So my flowers can finally bloom

I’ll protect them with every part of me
Even though there is not much left
But I will let the flowers bloom
Even though I’m afraid
That the thorns will destroy my heart
That I’ll lose myself again
But at least they were mine

The flowers
Something that will keep me from the dark
Something that will catch me if I fall

All these poems they’re mentioned in
Maybe these flowers
Were my words all along
They gave me life
When there was nothing but darkness
